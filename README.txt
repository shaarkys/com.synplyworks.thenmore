The Timer app adds the ability to temporarily turn on devices, by adding functionality to the Then... clause of your flows.

Start a flow with basically anything you like as a trigger in the When... clause.
Add a Timer-card to the Then... clause of your flow and select the desired functionality, like On/Off or Dim and enter the amount of time to change the device state.
You can also configure to not alter the device state, if the device was already on, while every re-trigger prolongs the timer.
When you have multiple flows activating the same device, you can also define to reset the duration to the current configured time, even when it's shorter than the time left in the running timer.
